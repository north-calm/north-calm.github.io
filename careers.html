Internships

Systems Programmer Intern for high-performance simulation software.

This is a focused internship for builders who enjoy low-level performance work, careful profiling, and clear systems reasoning.

Direct section link Share internship URL

What you will work on

Simulation Core Performance Engineering Systems Design

As a Systems Programmer Intern, your primary work will be on high-performance simulation software: improving runtime characteristics, refining data flow, and helping us push correctness and speed together.

  • Contribute to simulation modules with attention to memory and throughput.
  • Profile execution paths and remove bottlenecks with measurable outcomes.
  • Collaborate on architecture decisions that keep the system robust under scale.
  • Document tradeoffs and implementation choices with technical clarity.

What the internship experience looks like

  • Once you apply, we carefully review your application and project links.
  • If there is a fit, we contact you directly to get to know you better through a short conversation.
  • The internship duration is around 8 weeks, with clear milestones and hands-on engineering work.
  • Strong outcomes can open many opportunities, from capital rewards to company equity.
